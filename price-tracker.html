HOW TO READ PRICES

Two different signals

Weekly reference

A tracked market observation from the latest dataset. Useful for context, but not a live checkout quote.

Live retailer offer

An exact-model listing returned by the retailer data provider. Check the retailer page for final price, stock, shipping, tax, and seller terms.

No exact match

PCCheck leaves the product unpriced rather than assigning an offer to a similar model.

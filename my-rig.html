LOCAL SCAN / PRIVACY

Your hardware stays on your PC

My Rig requests a scan only when you press the button. The desktop app reads hardware information locally and returns it to this page. PCCheck does not upload the scan, store it in your account, or send it to an analytics service. Bluetooth names can identify devices nearby, so review the list before sharing a screenshot.

This is an inventory snapshot, not continuous temperature, fan-speed, clock-speed, or power monitoring. Device availability depends on Windows drivers and manufacturer reporting.

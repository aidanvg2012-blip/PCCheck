PCCHECK KNOWLEDGE BASE

Used PC buying guides

Practical checks before buying a used gaming PC or ordering custom parts.

01 / BEFORE YOU MEET

Questions for a used-PC seller

  • Ask for exact CPU, GPU, motherboard, RAM, storage, and PSU model numbers.
  • Ask when it was built and whether parts were repaired, replaced, or overclocked.
  • Request a recent video showing it booting and running a game or benchmark.
  • Confirm what accessories and operating-system license are included.
  • Ask if the seller will allow an in-person test before payment.
02 / TESTING

Inspect a used gaming PC

  • Confirm the system boots reliably and detects all RAM and storage.
  • Run a sustained game or benchmark and watch for crashes, artifacts, or shutdowns.
  • Check CPU and GPU temperatures under load.
  • Test USB ports, audio, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Ethernet, and display outputs.
  • Look for damaged ports, corrosion, missing screws, or unusual fan noise.
03 / COMPATIBILITY

Before ordering build parts

  • Confirm the exact CPU appears on the motherboard support list and check its required BIOS version.
  • Match DDR4 or DDR5 memory to the motherboard.
  • Check motherboard size against the case supported form factors.
  • Compare GPU length and thickness with case clearance.
  • Confirm cooler mounting, height, RAM clearance, and radiator support.
  • Check PSU model quality, wattage recommendations, and native GPU connectors.
  • Check M.2 slot type, PCIe generation, lane sharing, and SATA ports.
04 / PRICE CHECKING

How to judge a listing price

  • Use exact CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, motherboard, and PSU model names when checking current new-part replacement prices.
  • Compare similar complete systems with the same CPU and GPU generation.
  • Prefer recent sold listings over active asking prices when estimating used resale value.
  • Account for RAM, SSD, PSU quality, case, warranty, and condition.
  • Check local prices, shipping, taxes, and return protections.
  • Do not trust vague claims like “runs everything” without exact specs.
05 / REPORT LABELS

Understand PCCheck results

  • Conflict: entered details violate a basic compatibility rule.
  • Warning: a concern needs manufacturer or model-specific checking.
  • Not checked: information was missing or outside the checker scope.
  • Pass: that one comparison passed; it is not a whole-system certification.
  • Completeness: required listing fields supplied, not a performance score.
06 / SAFETY

Buying safely

  • Meet in a safe public place or suitable test location with a trusted adult.
  • Do not share passwords, verification codes, or financial details.
  • Use payment methods with buyer protection and follow platform rules.
  • Be cautious of pressure, unusual payment requests, or implausibly low prices.
  • For a high-value purchase, have an experienced adult or technician inspect it.

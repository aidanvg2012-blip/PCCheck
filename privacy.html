TRANSPARENCY

Privacy, accuracy
& limitations.

What this early PCCheck release does — and does not — do.

Data stored in your browser

Saved reports are stored in local browser storage on this device and browser. They are not uploaded to a PCCheck server and will not automatically appear on another device. Clearing browser storage may delete them. You can export saved reports as JSON.

Account beta and authentication

PCCheck account registration is a beta feature. If enabled, Supabase Auth processes email-and-password sign-in. PCCheck does not receive or store your plaintext password. Supabase processes your account email, account identifier, and authentication session. Account registration does not turn on report cloud sync: saved reports remain in the current browser and device. Do not create an account unless you have reviewed this information with a parent or guardian where appropriate. Account deletion and subscription self-service are not yet available in this beta, so do not use it for a paid subscription.

Payments are not live

The Plus checkout is restricted to Stripe test mode. Test checkouts do not charge real money and do not grant paid access until the subscription webhook and entitlement checks are verified. Do not enter real payment information while the product is in beta.

Clipboard and screenshot processing

The paste helper requests clipboard text only after you press its button; normal manual paste also works. Screenshot OCR loads Tesseract.js from jsDelivr when you choose to run it. The selected image is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a PCCheck server. The OCR library and language data are third-party resources, so this feature requires an internet connection.

Marketplace links

PCCheck recognizes the domain in a URL, but does not sign in to marketplaces, scrape listing details, or fetch private data. Specifications must be entered manually, pasted from copied listing text, or extracted from a screenshot using optional in-browser OCR. Future integrations must use permitted APIs or authorized data sources and respect marketplace terms.

How the checker works

The build checker compares values entered by the user. It can flag socket, memory generation, capacity, form-factor, and entered clearance mismatches. Power screening uses user-entered estimates and a simple planning margin. It does not inspect a real PC or identify every compatibility issue.

Always confirm exact model numbers, BIOS support, PSU quality and connectors, cooler mounting, GPU dimensions, storage lane sharing, and manufacturer requirements. A green result is not a guarantee.

Component market pricing

PCCheck can look up selected exact component models against Pricify (pricify.ai) weekly US retail price data. The lookup can show a typical new-retail price, the lowest tracked price, retailer information, and the dataset date for matched CPUs, GPUs, memory, SSDs, hard drives, motherboards, cases, and power supplies. The catalog is limited to selected popular products; broad descriptions and unmatched models are not assigned invented prices. The dataset is weekly, not a second-by-second price feed. Pricify data is used under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and a source link.

The combined component figure is a new-part replacement-cost reference, not the resale value of a used computer. Used value depends on age, condition, warranty, location, and verified recent sold listings. PCCheck does not currently access a complete used-market sold-price feed or guarantee a fair-value estimate. Always open the linked source and verify the price before buying.

Pricing and performance

The optional whole-PC comparison chart can compare asking prices you enter, but it does not verify that listings are equivalent or that they sold at those prices. PCCheck does not provide benchmark scores, frame-rate predictions, or seller reputation checks.

Independent project

PCCheck is not affiliated with Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist, OfferUp, AMD, Intel, NVIDIA, or any hardware manufacturer.

Before public launch

Before account registration or paid subscriptions are opened broadly, PCCheck needs a parent/guardian-reviewed privacy policy and terms, account deletion and data export controls, subscription cancellation/self-service, accessibility testing, and security testing. Do not enter sensitive personal information into PCCheck.
